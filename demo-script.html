Rejection Coach: Demo Call Script

Two people read this aloud: one plays the Rep, one plays the Customer. Put the laptop mic near the speaker. Cards in pink boxes appear on screen; do not read them aloud.

Script 1: The main demo (about 2 minutes)

Rep: Hi, this is Sam from T-Mobile. Do you have a minute? I'm calling to see if your current plan still fits you.
Customer: Uh, sure. But honestly, I'm happy with my current plan.
Card: Happy with my plan
Rep: That's good to hear. What do you use your phone for most?
Customer: Mostly streaming and maps.
Card: They shared something. It points to real plan features, such as a plan that includes Netflix.
Rep: Got it. What do you pay each month for your plan?
Customer: I'm on Verizon and I pay $90 a month for two lines, before taxes and fees, plan only.
Card: a verified comparison. It says honestly that T-Mobile Essentials 2.0 is $100 for two lines, $10 higher, and that this is service only, not total-bill savings.
Rep: Thanks, that helps me compare on the same basis.
Customer: Hmm. That's more than I pay now.
Card: Too expensive. It points to real features, with no invented discount.
Rep: Fair. What matters most to you besides the price?
Customer: How much is it per line?
Card: Customer asked, Price. The real standard prices from the catalog.
Rep: The price depends on how many lines you have. I'll confirm the exact standard price.
Customer: Is there a switching credit or something like that?
Card: it does NOT invent a credit. It says to check eligibility and the exact terms.
Rep: I'll check what applies to your account.
Customer: I'd have to talk to my wife first.
Card: I need to ask my spouse
Rep: Of course. Would tomorrow evening work for a quick call with both of you?
Customer: Maybe. Actually, can you just send me an email?
Card: Send me an email

Script 2: The stop card (about 15 seconds)

Rep: Hi, this is Sam from T-Mobile, calling about a new plan.
Customer: I'm not interested. Please stop calling me.
Red stop card: no push, confirm removal and end the call

Tips for the demo

  • Pause about a second between lines so each one becomes its own bubble.
  • Keep the rep's lines natural. They show off the Rep / Customer labels, and no card should appear on them.
  • If a bubble is labeled wrong, click it to switch between Rep and Customer.
  • Backup plan if the mic misbehaves: type the customer lines into the box at the bottom of the page.
Hackathon demo. Plans and prices come from the verified catalog in the Plan Database; the customers and calls are fictional.
